TECCI Park has successfully renewed its **LEED Silver certification** for Core & Shell through the U.S. Green Building Council (USGBC), reaffirming its position as Chennai's only LEED-certified green building available for lease on OMR.

## What this means

The recertification validates ongoing sustainability practices across:

- Energy efficiency operations
- Water conservation systems (9,00,000L rainwater storage)
- Waste management and recycling
- Indoor air quality monitoring
- Resource optimization

## Continuing commitment

Our upcoming rooftop solar installation (300 KW capacity) and wind energy procurement will further reduce grid dependency and continue our carbon-conscious operations.
